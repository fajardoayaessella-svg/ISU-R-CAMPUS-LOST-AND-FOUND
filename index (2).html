📦 How to Claim an Item

  1. Search for your missing item in the list.
  2. Click the Claim Item button.
  3. Provide your name and school email.
  4. Wait for the Lost and Found staff to verify your request.
  5. Present proof of ownership before collecting the item.

ℹ️ About This Website

The ISU Roxas Campus Lost and Found Management System is designed to help students and campus personnel report, search, and recover lost belongings. It provides an organized platform for managing lost and found reports within the campus.
